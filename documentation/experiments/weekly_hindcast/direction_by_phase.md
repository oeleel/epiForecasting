Share of scored rows (US excluded, every target week) where the median points the same way as what happened: each of `q50 - anchor` and `observed - anchor` is up, down or flat, the anchor being the last observed value at the forecast date. Flat means a change smaller than 10% of the anchor or 10 admissions, whichever is larger (an assumption; the repo defines no trend categories). Higher is better; Δ is config minus baseline in percentage points. `phase` is the target week's phase.

Reference: a forecast that always says flat (persistence) would agree on 39.3% of rows, the share whose truth is flat.
