Results in brief#

Baseline peak WIS 121.01, bias -58.8, coverage 0.855.

  • Loss weight on approaching-peak rows. Peak WIS 123.5-123.9 at every lambda (about +2.5, no improvement). Bias moves -35.7 / -17.9 / -6.4 / +7.9 for lambda 1.5 / 2 / 3 / 5. Coverage falls to 0.769 at lambda 5.
  • Calendar label instead of the curve label. Matches at matched bias: lambda 3 gives 122.66 with bias -6.2.
  • Training window 12 / 26 / 52 weeks. Peak WIS 238.26 / 240.24 / 197.99, worse at every phase.

Interpretation. The weight fixes the level, not the score: the median moves up but the intervals do not widen with it, so WIS does not improve and coverage drops. Short windows hurt every phase: the short-window rule is a per-series statistical-model rule and does not transfer to pooled XGBoost, which is the per-model-class point behind the new intake stub. Five baselines are identical, and the harness lands within 1.4 WIS of the 09-02 selection run.

Caveats

One split and one seed; calendar phases for scoring; K = 6 assumed; the weight acts on sample_weight only.

Open questions#

How should approaching-peak be defined, and what is K?

The experiment labels the K = 6 weeks before each eligible season's maximum. Is the definition right (relative to the max, or to a growth threshold), and should K be learned per season rather than fixed?

Are the 2020-2022 seasons admissible as training examples?

"Add examples from other seasons" needs a rule for which seasons count. The pandemic seasons have unusual shapes; the season-eligibility rule currently only requires 40 weeks of data.

What lambda range, and should it vary per season or per location?

The sweep used 1.5 to 5 with one global weight. A per-season or per-location lambda could follow the divergence of the current season instead of a fixed guess.

Warm-start versus retrain: a bank category or an experiment arm?

Re-fitting from the previous model versus training fresh at each cutoff changes what "shorter window" even means. It could be a model_characteristics entry or its own arm.

Calendar or curve-based phases for scoring?

Scoring uses calendar phases (Dec-Jan is peak). The curve-based segmentation (surge / plateau / decline) exists; switching the reward to it changes which entries match and which configuration wins.
